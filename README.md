# Cartomancie Intuitive

> An immersive tarot reading skill: traditional consultant posture, ethical guidelines, consultation ritual, and structured spreads (Marseille & Rider-Waite). Zero-context readings — no personal data, ever.

## What it is

**Cartomancie Intuitive** is a tarot reading skill that simulates an authentic card consultation while staying strictly neutral and ethical. It follows the posture of a traditional practitioner: a grounding exchange before the draw, the ritual cut of the deck, and structured spreads (5-card cross, timeline, or two-path choice). Each card is named and interpreted through its visual symbolism and its interactions with the others, with a grounding question to anchor the reading in the consultant's own experience.

## Core principles

- **Zero-context neutrality** — never uses prior knowledge, memory, or conversation history; the consultant is treated as a first-time visitor.
- **Ethics first** — no medical diagnoses, no predictions of death or disaster; difficult cards are reframed as transitions and necessary cycle breaks.
- **Interactive dialogue** — no top-down monologue; every step ends with a mirror question to anchor the symbols in the consultant's feelings.

## Consultation flow

1. **The entry threshold** — 2–3 preparatory questions (motive, priority area, nature of the expectation) before drawing anything.
2. **The ritual cut** — the consultant imagines cutting the deck with the left hand; two raw cards reveal the underlying energetic weather.
3. **The structured spread** — chosen to fit the question:
   - *5-card cross:* strength / obstacle / mental axis / concrete outcome / synthesis
   - *Timeline (3–4 steps):* 3–6 months / 6–12 months / 1–2 years / long-term anchor
   - *Two-path choice:* option A / option B / conciliation path
4. **Dialogue and grounding** — each step closes with a concrete anchoring question; deeper targeted draws available on request.
